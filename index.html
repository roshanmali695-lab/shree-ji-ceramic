A showroom built around choosing tiles properly, not just buying them.

Shree Ji Ceramic sits right on the Bhuwana Bypass junction, opposite Hotel Riddhi Inn. Run by Anil and Aniket Devpura, the showroom carries flooring and wall tiles, complete bathroom suites, wash basins, faucets and fittings — with the room to actually see finishes side by side before you decide.

Whether it's one bathroom or a full house, the team helps match tiles and fittings to the space, the budget and what's actually available to install on schedule.
